US Gateway Mathematics Outcomes Atlas

How many new students at each community college pass their first college-level math course, from state reporting, matched to federal facts about the college.

What the numbers mean

California uses Chancellor's Office metric 502C: of the students in a fall cohort, the share who completed transfer-level math in their first academic year, anywhere in the college's district.

Texas uses Coordinating Board measure C18UC: of first-time degree-seeking students, the share who passed a college-level math course with an A, B or C within two years. Texas reports students who arrived college-ready and not ready separately; the atlas adds them together and shows both parts.

Because the windows and cohorts differ, compare colleges within a state only. Map colors are fifths of each state's own colleges.

Read before using

  • Rates describe who enrolls as much as what a college does. They are not quality rankings.
  • Dallas, Lone Star, San Jacinto and Tarrant report as whole districts.
  • Dashed circles mark colleges with fewer than 30 students across the pooled cohorts.
